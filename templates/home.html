How CertVerify Works

Two powerful layers of security to ensure a document is exactly what it claims to be.

1. Hash-Chain Ledger Lookup

For certificates issued after onboarding, we check the unique digital fingerprint against our immutable ledger. If even a single character is altered, the signature fails instantly.

2. Forensic Image Analysis

For older documents or third-party scans, our OpenCV pipeline analyzes compression levels, metadata anomalies, and copy-move forgery to detect Photoshop tampering.
